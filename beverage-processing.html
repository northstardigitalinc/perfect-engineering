Beverage Processing

Beer & Brewery Juice & Soft Drinks Blending Systems

Overview

High-performance stainless steel processing plants for beer, juice, carbonated drinks, and beverage blending — from raw ingredient handling to final filling.

Our beverage processing systems combine hygienic design with precision process control. Every plant is built to food-grade and GMP standards with full CIP integration, PLC/SCADA automation, and validation documentation.

01 · Brewery Plant
Segment 01

Beer & Brewery
Processing Plant

Complete brewery processing solutions from malt milling through mashing, lautering, wort boiling, fermentation, maturation, and bright-beer tanks — engineered for craft and industrial-scale production.

→Mash Tun & Lauter Tun — SS 304 vessels with integrated sparging and false-bottom lautering
→Wort Kettle & Whirlpool — Steam-heated or direct-fire wort boiling with tangential whirlpool separation
→Fermentation Tanks — Jacketed cylindro-conical fermenters with glycol cooling and pressure control
→Bright Beer Tanks — CO₂-pressurised conditioning and carbonation tanks
→Yeast Management System — Collection, propagation, and pitching skid
→CIP Station — Automated multi-circuit cleaning for all vessels and transfer lines
Craft Brewery Lager & Ale Micro-Brewery Industrial Scale
02 · Juice & Soft Drinks
Segment 02

Juice & Soft Drink
Processing

Turnkey fruit juice extraction, blending, pasteurisation, and filling lines. Also covers carbonated soft drink blending systems with in-line proportioning, carbonation, and CIP-ready transfer piping.

→Juice Extraction & Clarification — Decanter centrifuge, UF membrane, and enzyme dosing systems
→Evaporator / Concentrator — Falling-film evaporation for concentrated juice production
→Pasteurisation — HTST plate or tubular pasteuriser with regeneration section
→Blending & Proportioning — In-line blending skid for syrup, water, CO₂, and flavour dosing
→Carbonation Unit — Plate carbonator with CO₂ pressure control for CSD applications
→Aseptic Buffer Tanks — Pre-fill holding tanks with sterile barrier and level control
NFC Juice Juice Concentrate CSD Blending Energy Drinks
03 · Blending Plant
Segment 03

Beverage Blending
Plant

Custom beverage blending plants for multi-ingredient formulations including dairy-based drinks, sports beverages, and ready-to-drink (RTD) tea and coffee. Designed for rapid flavour changeovers with full CIP capability.

→Ingredient Preparation Tanks — Jacketed mixing vessels with high-shear agitation for powder dissolution
→Multi-Stream Proportioning — Flow-meter controlled in-line blending for up to 6 streams
→Homogeniser — High-pressure homogenisation for emulsion stability
→UHT / Pasteurisation — Tubular or plate heat exchanger to meet shelf-life requirements
→Aseptic Filling Buffer — Sterile surge tanks ahead of filling lines
