Milk Powder Whey Powder Spray Drying

Overview

Precision-engineered drying systems for dairy and food-grade powder production — from evaporation through spray drying to packaging.

Our powder processing plants are designed for high-efficiency moisture removal and particle uniformity. Each system is engineered to meet ISO, GMP, and food safety standards, delivering consistent product quality batch after batch.

ISO 9001:2015 GMP Compliant CIP Ready PLC / SCADA
01 · Milk Powder Plant
Segment 01

Milk Powder Plant

Complete milk powder production lines incorporating multi-effect evaporators, spray dryers, fluid bed coolers, and powder conveying systems. Designed for whole milk, skimmed milk, and fortified powder variants.

→Multi-Effect Evaporators — Energy-efficient falling film evaporators for pre-concentration before spray drying
→Spray Drying Tower — Rotary atomiser or pressure nozzle designs achieving <4% residual moisture
→Fluid Bed Cooler / Agglomerator — Post-drying conditioning for instant-dissolve powder grades
→Fines Return System — Cyclone + bag filter for maximum yield recovery
→Powder Conveying & Sieving — Pneumatic conveying to silo or direct-to-bag packing
→CIP System — Automated cleaning-in-place for all wetted surfaces
Available Configurations
Whole Milk Powder Skimmed Milk Powder Fortified / Infant Grade Instant Powder
02 · Whey Powder Plant
Segment 02

Whey Powder Plant

Dedicated whey processing lines from raw liquid whey through nanofiltration, evaporation, spray drying, and powder packing. Produces sweet whey powder, demineralised whey, and whey protein concentrate (WPC).

→Whey Pre-treatment — Pasteurisation and fat removal to achieve feed-quality standardisation
→NF / RO Membrane Filtration — Demineralisation and lactose concentration for WPC grades
→Evaporation — Falling-film TVR or MVR evaporators for energy-efficient concentration
→Spray Drying — Nozzle-atomised drying with particle-size control
→Lactose Crystallisation — Optional crystallisation loop for permeate utilisation
→Automated Powder Bagging — 25 kg or 1000 kg big-bag filling stations
Output Grades
Sweet Whey Powder Demineralised Whey WPC 35 / WPC 80 Permeate Powder
03 · Yeast & Chemical Powder
Segment 03

Yeast & Chemical
Dry Products

Specialised drying systems for non-dairy applications including yeast, enzyme concentrates, and chemical dry products. Designed to handle heat-sensitive materials with low-temperature drying options.

→Low-Temperature Spray Drying — Inlet / outlet temperature control to preserve live cultures and enzymes
→Inert Gas Drying — Nitrogen-blanketed systems for oxygen-sensitive materials
→Paddle / Drum Dryers — For viscous or slurry feed materials unsuitable for spray drying
→ATEX-Rated Equipment — Explosion-proof design for flammable powder handling
→Multi-Stage Dust Collection — Cyclone + HEPA bag filters for zero-emission discharge
Application Types
Baker's Yeast Probiotic Cultures Enzyme Powders Chemical Dry Products
