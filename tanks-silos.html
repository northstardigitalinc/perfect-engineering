Tanks & Silos

Up to 300KL SS 304 / 316L Custom Design

Overview

High-capacity stainless steel storage tanks and silos from 1KL to 300KL — mirror-polished internal surfaces, full insulation options, and custom fittings for dairy, food, beverage, and chemical storage.

Every tank we fabricate is produced in our 59,400 sq. ft. facility using SS 304 or SS 316L plate, with 14-ton crane capacity for large-vessel assembly. All vessels are pressure-tested and finish-polished before dispatch.

01 · Vertical Silos
Segment 01

Vertical Storage
Silos

Large-capacity vertical silos for bulk milk, whey, water, CIP chemical, and raw material storage. Available with flat, conical, or dished bottoms in insulated or non-insulated configurations — up to 300KL capacity per unit.

→Capacity Range — 5KL to 300KL, single or multiple units with common header
→Material — SS 304 / SS 316L with 2B or mirror-finish internal surface
→Insulation — 60mm PUF / rockwool insulation with SS exterior cladding
→Agitation — Side-entry or top-entry agitator options for liquid blending
→Standard Fittings — Manway, CIP spray ball, vent, level gauge, thermometer, sample valve
→Legs & Support — Adjustable SS legs or skirt-base for foundation bolt installation

Horizontal Process
Tanks

Horizontal stainless steel process tanks for milk reception, cream maturation, buffer storage, and intermediate holding in processing lines. Designed with sloped bottoms and full drain capability for GMP compliance.

→Capacity Range — 1KL to 50KL in horizontal orientation
→Sloped Bottom — Minimum 2° slope toward drain port for complete emptying
→Jacketed Cooling — Dimple-plate or channel jacket for glycol / iced-water cooling
→Slow-Speed Agitator — Anchor or paddle agitator with top-drive gearbox
→Access Ladder & Platform — Integrated SS access ladder with safety handrail
